MCP Explorer 0.1.1

The first public release: a desktop workbench for MCP servers, on Windows and
Linux. (0.1.0 was tagged but never released: its Windows packaging failed.)

- Connects to stdio, Streamable HTTP and SSE servers on every revision from
  2024-11-05 to 2026-07-28, legacy and modern eras.
- Shows a server's whole surface (tools, prompts, resources, templates,
  instructions) and what it costs a model as context.
- Calls tools, gets prompts and reads resources through generated forms,
  with a confirm before anything not marked read-only; answers sampling,
  elicitation and roots by hand, never automatically.
- Keeps every byte on the wire, every call and every captured surface in an
  encrypted workspace, and diffs a server's surface across versions.
- Signs in to OAuth-protected servers, one step at a time, with tokens kept
  out of the wire log. Verified against a test authorization server; testing
  against hosted providers is under way.

Fixed since 0.1.0: packaging no longer fails when a GitHub token is in the
build environment.
